# Choose the visual metaphor for CEDIA's app mark

## Question

What original visual metaphor and construction should make CEDIA's human–AI co-creation
theme legible in a standalone app mark, with a calm, technically precise warmth and a
developer-directed AI partnership? Decide what the symbol should communicate at first
glance, and which visual shorthand or mistaken reading it must avoid.

## Comments

### Owner selections — interview rounds 1–2, 2026-09-25

- Core theme: a person and AI co-create.
- Brand character: confident and precise, with warmth.
- Relationship: the developer directs; AI contributes as a capable partner.
- Heritage: start from a clean-slate symbol, without retaining the existing `>|` silhouette.

### Owner response — interview round 3, 2026-09-25

- The mark should be one integrated form made from two interdependent parts.

### Owner confirmation — interview round 4, 2026-09-25

- The owner chose a letter-free emblem and approved moving into visual prototypes from
  the agreed brief: human–AI co-creation, with the developer directing a capable partner,
  expressed with confident technical precision and warmth.
- The exact visual metaphor and geometry remain open until the owner compares the prototypes.

### Owner response — AI brand language, 2026-09-25

- The owner has no specific metaphor in mind yet and likes AI brands generally. Use
  contemporary AI identity conventions as high-level inspiration, while keeping CEDIA's
  mark original and exploring distinct metaphors rather than copying a reference mark.
- The owner also rejected the three round-03 metaphors. A preferred visual reference within
  AI brands is still unknown; ask about a specific brand or the visual quality they like
  before choosing another metaphor.

### Owner reference — ChatGPT, 2026-09-25

- The owner named ChatGPT as the AI brand they like. Treat its identity as a reference for
  visual character, not a source shape to reproduce. The exact liked quality—interlaced mark,
  monochrome treatment, or overall balance—has not yet been specified.

### Owner specifies ChatGPT qualities — 2026-09-25

- The owner chose both an interlaced symbol and a restrained black-on-white presentation.
- Use these as high-level qualities for CEDIA's own two-part construction. Avoid the
  recognizable OpenAI knot topology and any direct reproduction of its logo.

### Owner adds Factory Droid — 2026-09-25

- The owner confirmed all three ChatGPT qualities from the clarification: interlaced mark,
  black-on-white restraint, and the overall brand feel. The owner also added Factory Droid
  as a reference.
- Working interpretation: preserve the two-part, interdependent brief in a monochrome,
  crafted mark with a precise software-tool character. Factory describes Droid as planning,
  building, and validating software while teams set its goals and policies; use that as
  product context, not as a source shape. See [Factory](https://factory.com/).

### Owner direction — circular silhouette, 2026-09-25

- The owner said the round-04 crossing studies still do not fit and clarified that the mark
  should have a circular overall silhouette, like the ChatGPT and Factory Droid references.
- Keep the high-level preferences already given: interlaced, black on white, and the overall
  ChatGPT character. The next study should explore circular two-part constructions rather
  than long open routes.
