## Question

For the clean-slate CEDIA app mark, should the primary symbol be a letter-free original
emblem, a CEDIA monogram, or an original emblem that only hints at the initial? Consider
recognition at small app-icon sizes and the distinctiveness of the mark.

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-09-25

The owner chose a letter-free original emblem for CEDIA's standalone app mark. The mark
will not use a CEDIA monogram or a C-shaped hint; any wordmark pairing remains a separate
presentation decision. This closes the letterform question while the mark's metaphor and
geometry remain open.
