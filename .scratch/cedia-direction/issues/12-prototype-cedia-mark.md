## Question

The owner selected the compact D-shaped fused-bloom silhouette and asked to integrate CEDIA's
former `>I` terminal mark, with both glyphs the same height. Three visual treatments were compared;
the owner approved A, using a bold, centered glyph pair. The adopted mark is monochrome, with an
inverted dark variant, and must remain legible at app-icon sizes. Trademark clearance is separate.
