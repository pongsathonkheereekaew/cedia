# CEDIA Twin Trace market scan — 2026-09-28

## Scope and verdict

At the time of this scan, I reviewed the editable Twin Trace SVG (`output/brand-prototypes/cedia-linework-proposal-2026-09-28/mark.svg`) and its rendered `output/brand-prototypes/cedia-linework-proposal-2026-09-28/preview.png`. Both files are absent from the current checkout; the following observations are historical, not a fresh inspection of those missing artifacts. The mark has two open `currentColor` paths, a 22-unit round stroke, round caps/joins, broad outer C-like arcs, and short inward turns with near-right-angle corners. The paths face one another around a central aperture; the icon is not a six-part radial weave.

The geometry is **moderately distinct from ChatGPT/OpenAI's Blossom**, but the broader visual category is crowded. At icon size, the first read can be “two Cs,” “a chain/link,” “an infinity-like loop,” or “a spiral,” depending on the background and rasterization. The asymmetric outer arcs and squared inner turns give CEDIA a useful point of difference, but they do not make the doubled-loop idea unique in the market. I found no exact match in this quick first-party scan; that is not evidence of availability, originality, or trademark clearance.

The requested ChatGPT-like quality is present as a visual language: heavy monochrome strokes, rounded terminals, controlled negative space, and a calm central void. OpenAI's own guide says its marks must be used exactly as provided and says not to incorporate them into a new mark or design a similar logo; CEDIA should therefore keep the reference at the level of finish and restraint, not Blossom construction ([OpenAI Design Guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/)).

## Distinctness assessment

- **Against OpenAI/ChatGPT:** medium distinction. Twin Trace's two paths and bilateral motion are clearly different from Blossom's six-fold interlock. The shared black/white rounded treatment and central negative space are broad style traits, not a safe basis for copying the Blossom.
- **Against major coding tools:** high distinction in this sample. Windsurf, Cline, and Copilot use materially different constructions or current lockup systems. Cursor's published cube system is also outside the loop family ([Cursor Brand](https://www.cursor.com/brand)).
- **Against the wider AI/app-icon market:** low-to-medium distinction. Two C-like strokes around a central void are a familiar logo grammar. The strongest differentiators are the unequal outer arcs, open terminals, and near-right-angle inner turns. Do not close the terminals, add a third/fourth loop, or make the paths perfectly symmetric; those changes would move it toward generic knot/double-C territory.

## Search resources and limits

No trademark clearance opinion was attempted. The following official tools are appropriate next checks:

1. Thailand DIP's [AI Image Search](https://tm-imagesearch.ipthailand.go.th/) accepts an uploaded image and reports preliminary visual similarity. DIP's February 2026 announcement says the tool is free, uses AI to score similarity, and is intended only as an initial self-check; it does not replace examination or counsel ([DIP release, PDF](https://ipthailand.go.th/images/26647/131__AI_Image_Search__TM_Checker_Final_18-02-69.pdf)).
2. WIPO's [Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) supports image-similarity searches across participating national and international collections. Its [FAQ](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/faqs_branddb) says uploaded images are processed confidentially and not retained, while its [availability guide](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/madrid-system/check-availability) warns that national coverage can be incomplete and recommends searching local registers and consulting a trademark professional.
3. The [USPTO design-search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/design-search-codes) says the federal system currently has no reverse-image search; a US search therefore requires design codes, text descriptions, goods/services, and related marks. It also warns that similarity can include appearance, meaning, and commercial impression.

This scan did not upload CEDIA's artwork to a trademark office, query every jurisdiction, inspect unregistered common-law use, or assess Nice classes. The result is a design triage record: Twin Trace is not an obvious Blossom copy, but its doubled-loop/spiral vocabulary is not a defensible claim of market uniqueness. Before public launch or filing, run the actual SVG/PNG through Thailand DIP and WIPO, conduct USPTO/EUIPO searches for the intended classes, and obtain counsel's clearance review.
